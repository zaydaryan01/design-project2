Hair Care Guide

Learn how to choose the right products for your hair type

Understanding Your Hair Type

Knowing your hair type is the first step to choosing the right products.

Straight
Smooth texture, oil spreads easily.
Wavy
Loose waves, can be frizzy or dry.
Curly
Defined curls, needs moisture.
Coily
Tight curls, very dry, needs deep hydration.

Why Hair Type Matters

Different hair types need different levels of moisture, strength, and care. Using the wrong products can lead to dryness, buildup, or damage.

How to Choose Products

  • Shampoo: Cleans scalp, choose gentle or moisturizing formulas.
  • Conditioner: Adds moisture and softness.
  • Leave-in: Extra hydration and protection.
  • Styling: Helps define or hold hairstyles.

Ingredients Explained

  • Sulfates: Strong cleansers, can be drying.
  • Silicones: Smooth hair but may cause buildup.
  • Natural oils: Add moisture and shine.
  • Protein: Strengthens weak hair.

Hair Care Tips

  • Don’t wash hair too often
  • Use lukewarm water
  • Apply conditioner to ends only
  • Use less product than you think you need

Common Mistakes

  • Copying someone else’s routine
  • Using too many products
  • Ignoring ingredient labels
  • Expecting instant results

Quick Guide

Match your hair type to product needs:

  • Fine hair: lightweight products
  • Dry hair: rich moisturizers
  • Oily hair: clarifying shampoos
  • Curly hair: curl creams & leave-ins
