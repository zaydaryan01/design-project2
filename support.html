Heres some questions that people usually ask

How to get rid of knots while avoiding breakage?

Some ways to get rid of knots while avoiding breakage :

  • You could wet your hair before trying to brush your hair
  • If that doesnt help much, you could put some oil on the part you wish to detangle or some leave in conditioner
  • Or you could use a detangling spray

Where to get some curly hair products in the west island?

  • Walmart: You can find good brands that are highly rated there but it may be a bit pricy and may not be much for curlier hair to coily hair.
  • Pharmaprix: You can find pretty good brands there but it is usually pretty expensive, like 20$ for a small bottle of curl cream.
  • Jean-Coutu: Same thing as pharmaprix.

How many times a week should I was my hair?

    Unlike straight hair, you shouldnt wash your hair like 4 times a week. With curly hair it takes longer for all the oils to travel down and its a lot more dry than straight hair. So if you have slightly curly hair like for example ; 2a-3a, you should wash it maybe 3-2 times a week and anything curlier, 1-2 times a week
