THE DIFFERENT TYPES OF HAIR

Type 1: (Straight): Lacks curls, waves, or volume, often appearing shiny but getting oily quickly.

Type 2: (Wavy): Ranges from loose loops to thicker, "S" shaped waves that may be frizzy.

Type 3: (Curly): Defined ringlets or spirals that are often dry and prone to tangling.

Type 4: (Coily): Extremely tight, dense curls or zig-zag patterns that experience significant shrinkage.

SOME GOOD HAIR OILS

Argan oil for shine and heat protection, Coconut oil for deep hydration and reducing protein loss, Jojoba oil for moisturizing the scalp without clogging pores. For growth, Castor oil improves hair thickness, while Tea tree oil soothes dandruff.
